Sus Out

Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Sus Out is a party game. The host runs the game on an iPhone. Other players can open a shared web page to see their own card. This policy describes what the app and that page do with information. Sus Out does not show ads, sell information, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Information that stays on the iPhone

The app stores these on the device:

  • Player names and scores you add in the game
  • The host name and discussion-timer choice
  • Which built-in word pairs have already been used
  • A random identifier used if you create custom word packs
  • A copy of custom word packs and which pairs have been played

In Game Settings, Clear Data & Reset Words deletes player profiles and marks built-in word pairs as unused again. That action keeps the host name and timer choice.

Cloud cards

If the host chooses QR cards, the app sends the game to a Cloudflare service so guests can open the shared link. That includes player display names, each player's role, and the secret words for that game.

The host's app deletes that game from Cloudflare when the session ends. The page does not ask guests to create an account. Anyone with the link can open it, which is how the table joins.

Custom word packs

Custom packs are optional. If you create one, the prompt you type and the generated pairs are sent to our pack service so the pack can be saved for that phone.

Generating a pack sends your prompt to Google Gemini, through our service, only to write the word pairs. Do not put secrets or someone else's private information in a prompt.

If you sign in with email, that email and password go to Supabase to create or open the account. Saved packs are stored with that account. The password is handled by Supabase Auth. The app keeps the sign-in session on the phone so you stay signed in.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, payment information, or advertising identifier. It does not use your information for tracking.

Children

Sus Out is a party game for groups playing together. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Asking about your information

To delete a custom-pack account and its packs, open Game Settings, sign in, and tap Delete account. To ask a question, open an issue on the public project page: github.com/kotinarupali/mismatch-ios/issues/new.
